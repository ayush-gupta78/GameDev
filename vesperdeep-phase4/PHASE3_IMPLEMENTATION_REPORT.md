## Systems and approved rules

- `scripts/controls.gd`: semantic keyboard/controller actions, runtime rebind/conflict rejection, restore defaults and fixed menu fallbacks. Remaps do not survive relaunch; Phase 2 `implementation-handoff.md` §10 requires remapping where practical but does **not** mandate persistence.
- `scripts/player_tuning.gd`, `player.gd`, `player_combat.gd`: acceleration/deceleration, buffered/coyote jump, variable height, wall slide/jump, one-air-use Echo Step, owner-approved one-use Double Jump unlocked with the C3 Sigil (tunable separate upward impulse and original wing flash), three-direction slash, pogo, immunity/knockback, hazard recovery; three valid hits per charge, max three, excess partial hits discarded; stationary uninterrupted 1.1-second Focus grants +1 HP at cost of one charge; interruption preserves charge. Values not specified by the GDD are prototype calibration, not verified Hollow Knight numbers.
- `scripts/enemy_profile.gd`, `enemy.gd`, `test_room.gd`: Drudge, Needle and Moth behavior, warnings, active threats, knockback, death, visible Needle projectile. Position is assigned **before** `add_child` calls enemy `_ready`, so origin/home derives from the actual spawn; Moths now hover 15 units above spawn and can be reached by a grounded up-slash during committed dives in G1/G2. Ordinary enemy deaths and partial HP are session-local, preserved between rooms until death/rest, but intentionally not across app relaunch.
- `scripts/miniboss_prototype.gd`: only Warden/Cantor progression integration fixtures and atomic reward groups. Their complete choreography, final art/audio and encounter-quality sign-off are outside Phase 3.
- `scripts/test_room.gd`, `main.gd`: 12 connected prototype rooms, gates and mandatory dialogue, HUD, camera limits, visibly labeled blue shrines, safe ledge and death respawn, G4 return to G3. M4 has a continuous boss floor and temporary end walls; its mandatory Echo Step gap is beyond the fight deck, already present before victory. Godot `SceneTree.paused` freezes pausable player/room/combat/projectiles/tweens during pause and dialogue, while main stays `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS` for UI. Normal deaths preserve durable flags and return to selected shrine; unfinished G4 loads/deaths restart from G3. G4 has no final boss yet.
- `scripts/run_state.gd`, `player.gd`: versioned validated JSON and atomic write/rename; ability/gate flags, checkpoints, HP/charges/partial-hit counter and distinct rest/death rules. C3 defeat now atomically saves `double_jump_owned` with the Sigil. Save version 2 accepts/migrates valid version-1 saves, deriving the new flag from prior Cantor victory. Nonfatal damage immediately saves changed HP; fatal damage invokes shrine death, which saves its own reset.

## Defects corrected after the original baseline

1. Opaque room rendered over the player. The room is now ordered before player/UI; regression assertion and OpenGL pixel check pass.
2. Wall jump velocity went away from wall while facing turned toward it. Facing now follows the wall normal; collision/regression check passes.
3. Mandatory locked dialogue exposed an immobilized player to enemy harm. Player damage is refused while locked and enemy/miniboss physics timers suspend during dialogue/pause; regression check passes.
4. Walking through the Sigil seal did not record the durable `glass_gate_open` flag. C4→G1 now commits it without optional interaction; regression check passes.
5. Partially injured ordinary enemies silently regained full HP on room re-entry. Run-local HP ledger now persists wounds until death/rest and clears on app relaunch, matching the approved reset rule; regression check passes.
6. Choir Needle shots had collision but no rendered body. Added a small visible polygon; scene-frame color check passes.
7. Glass Moth committed-target dive recalculated direction after overshoot and reversed. It now retains the committed direction through active phase; frame-driven repro passes.
8. Enemy `_ready` captured `(0,0)` before spawn positioning, sending Moths toward the wrong origin. All archetypes now receive positions before joining the room tree; all six tested spawn/home pairs and Moth recovery path pass.
9. Input-only lock left player gravity/dash, attack timers and Needle tweens running during pause/dialogue. Native `SceneTree.paused` now freezes gameplay; only main UI remains processable. Midair, active dash, projectile, Focus, hazard and routed menu/dialogue resume checks pass without a softlock.
10. Nonfatal HP damage changed the in-memory value but not disk until a later action. Nonfatal hits now save immediately; an independent-process write/damage/exit/relaunch test restores 3 HP from a previously saved 4 HP. Fatal shrine-save path remains unchanged.
11. M4's original practice gap lay inside the Warden's combat deck; a rightward charge fell through and never recovered. A temporary grate prevented that fall but disappeared on victory, exposing a strange new hole. On owner direction, M4 now has a continuous x0–825 boss floor, temporary end walls and a pre-existing transition dash gap x825–1020 behind the right wall. Warden spawns centrally; shared recoil drag stops Warden/Cantor drifting to room edges. Falling/displacement with remaining boss HP does not emit victory; only ten accepted combat strikes defeat Warden. Echo Step and the route commit together; an actual jump+dash crosses to C1. No RB workaround. Corrected Linux gameplay was subsequently confirmed by the owner.
12. G1/G2 Moths returned to an unreachable hover height; only their home offset changed, from 55 to 15 units above spawn. Both G2 Moths and the G1 Moth can now be hit and defeated by up-slash during their dives. C1's light-blue block is the approved shrine, now visibly labeled with E/RB rest instructions; it was not removed or converted to a platform.
13. Owner-approved Double Jump after C3 uses one additional press, distinct impulse, landing/wall-jump reset and temporary wing-like lines. Its C3 unlock is saved with the Sigil; existing v1 saves migrate. No room, boss or narrative change was introduced.
