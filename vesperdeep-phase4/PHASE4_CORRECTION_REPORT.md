## Root causes and fixes

1. **Unwanted wall sticking:** `player.gd` previously applied wall-slide speed and wall-jump whenever `is_on_wall()` returned true, including ordinary ledges and boundaries. The movement loop now checks the actual horizontal slide collider for group `climbable_wall`. Only the two vertical M3 chimney `StaticBody2D` stones receive that group; the horizontal top and other walls do not. Ordinary C1 boundaries fall at normal gravity. No change to coyote time, buffered jump, Double Jump, dash or floor collision.
2. **Flying enemy sticking:** Moths previously collided physically with the player (`collision_mask=3`) while moving via `move_and_slide()`, allowing a dive or return-home movement to press into the player's body. Enemy masks now collide with terrain only (`1`), and damage is delivered through the existing explicit active-hit logic. The committed Moth dive and entire warning line reach the warned target; a successful hit interrupts the dive into a short recoil/recovery; home recovery finishes before the next warning. Same-kind sibling attacks are staggered. On non-attacking overlap, the same contact handler separates without inventing damage.
3. **Contact knockback/stagger:** `Vessel.receive_damage()` used a facing-based fixed recoil and enemies did not recoil on hitting the player. The new `receive_body_contact()` path keeps existing HP, save, Focus interruption and 0.85-second invulnerability behavior, but derives horizontal separation from actor positions (relative velocity resolves exact co-location). Both bodies recoil; player tuning has independent `contact_knockback=235`, `contact_lift=125`, `contact_stagger_seconds=0.10`, while enemy profiles have `contact_knockback=155`, `contact_stagger_seconds=0.24`. Timers decay during physics, with terrain collisions preserved; player input is reduced slightly during recoil, not disabled, so jump/dash remain available. Only accepted contact starts a new stagger. Invulnerable contacts and simultaneous second enemies cannot restagger or damage continuously. Passive body overlap reacts without HP loss; weapon hits and Needle projectiles retain their separate original damage paths. Toll Warden has resistant `contact_knockback=45`, `contact_stagger_seconds=0.13`; its HP, hit windows, arena floor and victory/ability flags do not depend on collision displacement. These are prototype tuning knobs, not measured reference-game values.
4. **Encounter density:** Curated existing-archetype placements: M1 one Drudge after the safe movement introduction; M2 two Drudges between the shrine and Edda/Pip but outside their safe patches; M3 two Drudges plus one Moth away from the optional chimney/latch; M4 one Drudge on the approach that disappears without reward once the solo Warden activates. The owner's correction explicitly supersedes the earlier GDD's zero-enemy M2/two-enemy-only M3 placements; no design document, route, room or enemy archetype was added.
5. **Regular difficulty:** Drudges turn/pursue within a 95-pixel home leash, telegraph only in melee range, stagger same-kind warnings and strike windows, and use a still-readable 0.48-second warning and 0.52-second recovery. Their calibrated speed/detection are 78 px/s and 220 px. Moths commit to readable dives and return before the next attempt. HP and attack damage are not inflated. Enemy contact recoil briefly interrupts aggression, preventing dragging/trapping without changing weapon-hit responses.
6. **Toll Warden difficulty:** Existing corrected behavior now closes distance in idle, replaces an out-of-range sweep with a marked avoidable drop, travels through its advertised shove, reduces idle downtime below half HP and reacts to repeated close-range punishes with an alternate telegraphed shove. The new bounded contact recoil prevents pinning and preserves the continuous arena and HP-depleting reward. Warning and recovery windows, three patterns, harmless intro, ten-HP pool, one-point damage and Echo Step/relay/exit flags remain.

The approved approximate **17-minute Mourning Shore** and **60-minute full-game** targets are unchanged. No lengthening sections or artificial waits were added; neither target was measured in the corrected build.
