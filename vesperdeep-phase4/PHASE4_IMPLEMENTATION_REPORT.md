# Phase 4 implementation report — corrected owner-review candidate

Status: implemented and independently script-tested on Linux; corrected build awaits owner verification. The owner manually tested the preceding `vesperdeep-phase4-review.zip` on Linux, reported that the rest of the game functioned as expected, and requested corrections including body-contact recoil/stagger. This report does not promote those corrections to owner PASS. The approved Phase 2 GDD, Phase 3 source, and both original review/input archives remain unchanged. The approved ~17-minute Mourning Shore target is retained but has not been measured on the corrected build.

## First-region route and targeted corrections

M1 Wreckfall → M2 House of Kept Names → M3 Ossuary Steps → M4 Tollhouse → dash-gated C1 Sluice Landing is unchanged. Three M1 practice platforms, shallow reversible drop, unfarmable hanging target, M2 shrine and mandatory Edda/Pip exchange, M3 optional chimney/upper inscriptions and use-required latch, continuous M4 boss floor, separate Echo Step culvert and C1 shrine remain. Beyond C1, C2–G4 are retained Phase 3 regression scaffolds, not authored Phase 4 regions. No extra rooms, travel sections, time gates or Phase 5 content were added.

1. **Wall behavior:** Player wall slide and wall jump now require contact with an actual StaticBody2D in group `climbable_wall`. Only the two vertical M3 chimney stones have that tag; ordinary room boundaries and platform edges still collide but do not arrest falling or offer a wall jump. Original dash, Double Jump, knockback, gravity and route traversal stay intact. Tests cover synthetic ordinary/marked walls and actual M3 slide and C1 boundary fall.
2. **Flying enemy contact:** Glass Moth bodies collide with terrain but no longer physically pin the player; an active warned dive still delivers a single valid damage event via its existing hit logic. Dive duration reaches the committed target, warning line spans that entire path, recovery returns to the recorded hover point before re-engagement, and sibling Moths stagger their telegraph/attack/recovery turns. Existing G1/G2 and new M3 Moth checks cover paired and mixed encounters.
3. **Encounter density and ground AI:** New deliberately positioned encounters are M1 one Paper Drudge after the movement introduction; M2 two Drudges in a central combat patch away from shrine and Edda/Pip; M3 the original two Drudges plus one Glass Moth away from the chimney and latch; M4 one pre-arena Drudge before the Warden activation line, despawned without reward at boss activation so there is no simultaneous boss/mob threat. The newer owner correction explicitly supersedes the GDD's original zero-enemy M2 and two-enemy M3 placements; the GDD files themselves were not altered. No new archetype, HP inflation or damage inflation was introduced. Drudges now turn toward nearby players, pursue within a bounded home area, attack only within melee reach and alternate active windows with same-kind siblings. Their warning remains visible (0.48 s), recovery remains punishable (0.52 s); speed/detection were calibrated to 78 px/s and 220 px. Roam bounds keep NPC/shrine patches and the M4 trigger clear. Focus, enemy HP persistence, rest/death reset, stun immunity and normal combat flags remain as before.
4. **Toll Warden:** It approaches distant players during idle, switches an out-of-range sweep to a premarked drop, and drives its shove through most of its visible warning length. Later half-HP intervals shorten idle while retaining the already established warning/recovery windows; repeated close-range punish positioning can prompt a visibly telegraphed shove instead of a static loop. The original three patterns, ten-HP pool, one-point damage, harmless three-tap introduction, bounding walls, fair committed marker, legitimate HP-depleting defeat and atomic Echo Step/relay/exit reward are unchanged. No spoken boss dialogue was added.
5. **Narrative/save foundation:** S01–S05 intro, Edda/Pip, gravestone/Rell, post-Warden Listener line, interruption/relaunch, v1/v2→v3 save migration, checkpoint/death and C1 objective remain. The separate Phase 4 user-data name protects Phase 3 saves.
